-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE SHORT VERSION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Building the full user hierarchy takes about 6 to 8 weeks with one developer,
or about 4 weeks with two.

Everything else still needed to reach a live, production V1 takes about
12 to 16 weeks on top of that with one developer.

All up: roughly 4.5 to 6 months with one developer, or 2.5 to 3.5 months with
two developers plus part-time testing.

The most important point is not the org chart itself. It is the access control
that comes with it. Right now any admin user can see every safety concern and
every person in EVERY business on the platform. Until that is fixed we can only
safely run one company. That single issue is what blocks signing up a second
customer.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

More is built than the old plan document suggests. Today the app has:

  * Worker to Supervisor link. A worker picks their supervisor at sign-up and
    their concerns route to that person. This works.

  * A Company record per NZBN business.

  * Four role names in the system (Platform Owner, Company Owner, Supervisor,
    Worker), squashed down to three in the app (Worker, Supervisor, Admin).

  * A basic admin Users screen - list people, add a person, delete a person.

  * Working login, concern reporting, supervisor responses, closing the loop,
    notifications and analytics.

In short: a flat two-level structure (worker under supervisor) plus a company
name attached to each person. That is roughly 40% of a real hierarchy.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT IS MISSING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1  The Company Owner level does not exist at all.
     The role name is defined, but nothing in the system treats it differently.
     A Company Owner is currently handed full platform admin access.

3.2  There is no separation between companies.
     An admin sees all concerns from all businesses, and all analytics across
     all businesses. There is no "you only see your own company" rule anywhere
     in the system. This is the single biggest gap.

3.3  Sites and crews are just text.
     "Crew" is a free-text box on a person. "Sites" is only a number on the
     company. There is no real structure, so we cannot route by site, move a
     worker between crews, or hand a crew over when a supervisor leaves.

3.4  There is no way to manage the relationships once they are set.
     No approve, no reject, no reassign a worker to a different supervisor,
     no invite, no deactivate. A supervisor awaiting approval is currently
     invisible in the app and there is no screen to approve them.

3.5  A minor security leak.
     The list of supervisors is served without requiring login, and it is not
     filtered by company. Anyone can read every supervisor's name and crew.

  Database changes: real Site and Crew records, the Company Owner

  and every report ...............................................  4 - 5 days

  reassign, an approvals queue, search and paging ................  4 - 5 days

  Testing that every role sees exactly what it should and
  nothing more ...................................................  3 - 4 days

  TOTAL .........................................................  27 - 38 days

5. EFFORT - EVERYTHING ELSE STILL OUTSTANDING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One developer, working days.

  Login and sign-in
    Move to Circle single sign-on when it is ready ...............  5 - 8 days
    (Or 2 - 3 days to harden our own login instead. Worth noting: the
    current login reads every user account out of the database on each
    attempt, and there is no limit on repeated password guesses.)

  Photos
    Move photos out of the database and into proper file storage,
    with virus scanning and location data stripped ...............  5 - 7 days
    (Photos are currently stored inside the database as text, which
    will not scale.)

  Working offline
    Hold reports on the phone with no signal and send them
    automatically when signal returns ............................ 8 - 10 days
    (The server side is ready. The phone side is not built.)

  Push notifications .............................................  4 - 5 days
    (Nothing built yet.)

  Live NZBN business lookup ......................................  3 - 4 days
    (Currently searches our own database, not the government register.)

  Analytics hardening: company filtering, a minimum group size so
  small teams cannot be identified, and CSV / Excel / PDF export ..  6 - 8 days

  Privacy review of anonymous reporting, and support for the
  formal privacy assessment ......................................  3 - 5 days

  Automated tests ................................................ 8 - 10 days
    (There are currently no tests at all.)

  Deployment pipeline, environments, secret storage, monitoring ...  6 - 8 days

  New Zealand hosting setup ......................................  3 - 5 days
    (Plus lead time for procurement.)

  Security review and fixing what it finds .......................  5 - 8 days

                                                (12 - 16 weeks, one developer)


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. IMPACT - WHY THIS MATTERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Privacy and compliance
    This is ACC-funded and covered by the Privacy Act 2020. Today an admin
    account can read every worker's safety concern in every business. That is
    the highest risk item in the whole system. It is not a hypothetical - it
    is how the code behaves right now.

Commercial
    We cannot sign up a second business until company separation is in place,
    because their data would be visible to each other's admins. This is a yes
    or no gate on rollout, not a nice-to-have.

Effect on existing work
    This change takes things away rather than adds them - people who can see
    everything today will see less afterwards. That means existing data has to
    be migrated and every screen re-tested. It cannot be quietly switched on
    behind a flag the way a new feature can.

Effect on the app
    The Company Owner level is a whole new fourth experience in the app -
    its own menu, its own dashboard, its own version of the reports.

Risk of wasted work
    If Circle ends up owning all accounts and the reporting structure, as the
    original plan assumed, then roughly a third of the hierarchy work - the
    invite, approval and user-creation screens - gets thrown away. The company
    separation and permission work survives either way.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. RECOMMENDATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Split the work in two and put the Circle decision in the middle.

  STAGE A - about 3 weeks
      Company separation, the permission rebuild, supervisor assignment and
      approval, and closing the supervisor list leak.
      This is worth doing whatever happens with Circle, and it closes the
      privacy gap.

  DECISION POINT
      Confirm whether Circle is going ahead, and whether Company Owner needs
      its own experience.

  STAGE B - about 3 to 5 weeks
      Company Owner experience, sites and crews, and account creation - but
      only the parts Circle will not be handling.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. QUESTIONS FOR THE CLIENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Is Circle definitely going ahead, and when will it be ready?
     This decides whether we build account management or just read from theirs.

  2. Does a Company Owner need their own screens, or is treating them as an
     admin acceptable for the first release?

  3. Do we need site-level detail, or is crew-level enough?

  4. Is New Zealand data hosting a hard requirement?

  5. Are photos definitely in the first release?
